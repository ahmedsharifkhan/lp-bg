FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about forming your US LLC with Business Globalizer.

Do I need to be a US citizen or resident?

No! Anyone from anywhere in the world can form a US LLC. We specialize in non-resident formations and have helped entrepreneurs from 50+ countries successfully launch their US businesses.

How long does the formation take?

Most formations complete within 3–5 business days after we receive your documents. Elite package clients receive priority processing for the fastest possible turnaround.

What is the State Fee?

Each US state charges a separate government filing fee paid directly to the state. This varies by state (typically $50–$300) and is not included in our service fee. We'll inform you of the exact amount before you proceed.

Do I need a US address?

No. All our packages include a registered agent service that serves as your official US address for legal correspondence. The Growth package additionally includes a full business address for 1 year.

Can I open a US bank account remotely?

Yes! We guide you through the entire process. Our Growth and Elite packages include active bank account opening support, helping you get set up with US banking options compatible with non-resident LLCs.

Is my personal information secure?

Absolutely. We use bank-level encryption for all data transmission and storage. We never share your personal information or business details with third parties under any circumstances.
