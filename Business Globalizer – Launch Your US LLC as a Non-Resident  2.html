FAQ

Common Questions Answered

Everything you need to know about forming your US LLC as a non-resident.

Do I need to be a US citizen or resident to form a US LLC?

Absolutely not. Any person from virtually any country in the world can own and operate a US LLC. You don't need a Social Security Number, a US address, or even to visit the United States. Business Globalizer specializes specifically in non-resident LLC formation.

What is an EIN and why do I need one?

An EIN (Employer Identification Number) is your LLC's federal tax ID, issued by the IRS. You need it to open a US bank account, set up Stripe or PayPal, file taxes, hire employees, and sign business contracts. As an IRS CAA Certified Agent, we can obtain your EIN directly — no SSN required.

How long does the entire process take?

We start processing your LLC the same day we receive your application. LLC formation typically takes 3–7 business days depending on your state. The EIN takes an additional 2–4 weeks when obtained through our CAA certification. Bank account setup is done within 1–3 days after EIN receipt. Total: approximately 3–6 weeks for the complete package.

Which US state should I form my LLC in?

We most commonly recommend Wyoming or Delaware for non-residents. Wyoming has no state income tax, low annual fees, strong privacy protections, and no minimum capital requirements. Delaware is preferred if you plan to raise venture capital or deal with US institutional investors. We'll recommend the best state for your specific situation during your free consultation.

Will I have to pay US taxes as a non-resident LLC owner?

In most cases, if your LLC is single-member, foreign-owned, and has no US-based employees or physical operations ("effectively connected income"), you will not owe US income tax. However, there are annual IRS filing requirements (Form 5472 + 1120). We strongly recommend consulting a tax professional — we can refer you to qualified international tax advisors.

What's included in your service — are there hidden fees?

No hidden fees — ever. Your package includes: LLC formation (state filing fees included), EIN obtainment via our CAA status, 1 year of registered agent service, US business address, bank account setup assistance, and Stripe/PayPal onboarding guidance. Everything is quoted upfront. Book your free consultation to get an exact quote for your situation.
